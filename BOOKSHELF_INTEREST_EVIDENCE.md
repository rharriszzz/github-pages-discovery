## Candidate interest structure

Treat these as **candidate nodes** to be reviewed, rather than as new, confirmed user interests. Distinguish subjects from techniques and application domains.

### Core subject clusters suggested by multiple books

1. **Mathematical physics and geometry:** Carroll, Wald, Bishop/Goldberg, Arnold, Landau/Lifshitz and Riley/Hobson/Bence. Candidate connections: manifolds, tensors, curvature, Hamiltonian systems, differential equations, general relativity.
2. **Mathematical methods and approximation:** Bender/Orszag, Strang, Riley/Hobson/Bence, Arnold. Candidate connections: asymptotics, perturbation theory, Fourier analysis, analytical vs numerical methods, stability of approximations.
3. **Physics and engineering design:** Robust Control, Art of Electronics, Composite Basics, Landau/Lifshitz. Candidate connections: feedback, dynamics, hardware implementation, physical materials, measurement.
4. **Abstract mathematics:** Frenkel/Lepowsky/Meurman, with links to modular forms, representation theory and monstrous moonshine. This cluster is narrow but potentially deep.
5. **Information and intellectual history:** *A Mind at Play* is about Claude Shannon; consider information theory, coding, uncertainty and the history of mathematical/electronic ideas.

### Cross-links worth exploring (hypotheses, not evidence of preference)

- **Control + dynamical systems + numerics:** robust stabilization and feedback for uncertain mechanical or fluid systems; also applications to simulation and robotics.
- **General relativity + tensor analysis + geometry:** the mathematics behind curvature and space-time, with possible educational visualization or computational notebooks.
- **Arnold + Bender/Orszag + numerical fluid dynamics:** symplectic methods, nonlinear dynamics, perturbation limits and verification of simulations.
- **Electronics + information theory:** measurement, noise, signals, coding and instrumentation; possible connections to music-related engineering projects.
- **Composite materials + controls + space systems:** structures and control of aerospace systems, but do not imply a confirmed aerospace interest from these books alone.
- **Vertex operator algebras + formal mathematical computation:** mathematical structure and rigorous proof; any link to AI theorem proving is an adjacent research *hypothesis*.
